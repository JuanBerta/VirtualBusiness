Business Simulation Game

Welcome to Business Sim!

Goal: Your objective is to make a profit by strategically buying products from suppliers, selling them to wholesalers, or fulfilling contracts.

How to Play:

  • Review available products from Suppliers and buy what you think you can sell or use for contracts.
  • Check what Wholesalers are demanding and sell your products to them.
  • Look at Market Contracts. If you can fulfill one by its deadline, accept it for a potentially higher profit.
  • Manage your inventory and money carefully.
  • Click "Advance Turn" to move to the next day, which will update markets, prices, and contract deadlines.

Note: If you encounter issues or the game doesn't display correctly, check the browser's Developer Console (usually F12) for detailed logs and error messages.
